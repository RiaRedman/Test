OUR STORY

Authentic Italian Craft, 100% Halal

Founded in central Manchester, Verde & Grano was created with a simple belief: everyone should be able to experience traditional, hand-rolled Italian pasta culture without compromising on dietary values. Every morning, our kitchen extrudes and shapes fresh durum wheat pasta from scratch, pairing slow-simmered sauces with HMC-certified Halal meats and vegetarian-rennet cheeses. Instead of wine and liqueurs, our bar crafts zero-proof botanical spritzes infused with Mediterranean citrus and fresh herbs—delivering vibrant, modern casual dining in an alcohol-free space.
