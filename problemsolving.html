Problem Solving Case Study

CTO Business Context: Verde & Grano Digital Launch

As Chief Technology Officer (CTO) for Verde & Grano, a 100% Halal, alcohol-free Italian pasta and mocktail bar in central Manchester, I designed this web prototype to give the restaurant a clean digital home before launch. Previously, customers would have to call up or DM on social media just to ask if the food was Halal, check opening times, or book a table—which slows things down and leads to missed bookings. My goal was to put everything in one place: clear Halal assurance right at the top, an easy-to-browse menu, instant table booking, and an automated inquiry email generator that runs directly in the browser without needing an expensive backend server.

Strategic Design Choices & Technical Rationale

User Journey & Conversion Optimisation (UX):

The layout was designed to minimise friction for the user. By anchoring the navigation bar with a high-contrast 'Book A Table' call-to-action button, visitors can instantly trigger an interactive drop-down drawer, allowing for easy access.

Colour Psychology & Brand Differentiation:

Abandoning generic fast-casual tropes, the palette pairs warm terracotta, earthy olive green, soft cream, and rich espresso brown. This creates a sophisticated, editorial atmosphere that speaks directly to urban diners seeking premium, Halal Italian dining.

Modular JavaScript Interactivity:

To deliver a seamless application feel without heavy front-end frameworks, vanilla JavaScript drives the dynamic menu tab switcher (instantly filtering dish categories and high-definition photography without page reloads) and powers the interactive contact email generator with one-click clipboard copying.

Accessibility & Compliance

The prototype follows WCAG best practices by using semantic HTML5 tags and explicit form labeling for screen-reader compatibility. Furthermore, it ensures GDPR compliance through a persistent cookie banner backed by browser localStorage and a dedicated settings toggle that allows users to manage their privacy preferences at any time.

Mobile-First Layout:

Because most people look up restaurants on their phones, I built the sections with responsive CSS Grid and Flexbox layouts so the booking drawer, menu tabs, and forms stay easy to tap and read on any screen size.

OUR STORY

Authentic Italian Craft, 100% Halal

Founded in central Manchester, Verde & Grano was created with a simple belief: everyone should be able to experience traditional, hand-rolled Italian pasta culture without compromising on dietary values. Every morning, our kitchen extrudes and shapes fresh durum wheat pasta from scratch, pairing slow-simmered sauces with HMC-certified Halal meats and vegetarian-rennet cheeses. Instead of wine and liqueurs, our bar crafts zero-proof botanical spritzes infused with Mediterranean citrus and fresh herbs—delivering vibrant, modern casual dining in an alcohol-free space.
